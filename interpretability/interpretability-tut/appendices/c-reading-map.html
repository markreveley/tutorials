Reading map

Shorter routes through the course for three purposes.

Reviewing someone else’s interpretability claim (90 minutes)

Lessons 00, 01, 03, 05, and 13. Then apply the checklist in lesson 13 to the claim.

Replicating a vision claim on a laptop (half a day)

Lessons 01, 06, and 07; the template; labs/curve_detector.py and its tests.

Explaining a language-model neuron (a day)

Lessons 02 to 05, 12, 13, and the capstone; labs/protocol.py and labs/explanation_score.py.
