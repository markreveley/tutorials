Module 3 · Language methods on GPT-2 small

Causal interventions: activation patching with a clean/corrupted pair

Correlational evidence says a component carries information; interventional evidence says the model uses it. Patching the residual stream of GPT-2 small, with a pre-registered claim and a control position.

Represents versus uses

Everything so far has been correlational: a neuron’s activation co-varies with a rule, a probe reads a label, a component’s output projects onto a token. Huang et al. frame the gap as two modes: the observational mode “only evaluates whether a concept is encoded, as opposed to used,” and the intervention mode construes an explanation E of neuron a “as a claim that the neuron a is a causal mediator of the concept denoted by E” [Huang et al. 2023]. Their audit of GPT-4’s explanations of GPT-2 XL neurons found that in the intervention mode, “we are unable to find evidence that neurons are causal mediators of the concepts denoted by the explanations.” Explanations that scored well observationally did not, in their tests, correspond to neurons the model relied on.

Activation patching is the basic interventional tool. Take two inputs that differ in one respect the claim is about: a clean prompt that produces the behaviour and a corrupted one that does not. Run the corrupted prompt, but at one layer and one position replace the activation with its value from the clean run. If the behaviour comes back, the information the claim is about passed through that activation.

The run

Clean: “The capital of France is”; corrupted: “The capital of Italy is”. Metric: logit(“ Paris”) − logit(“ Rome”) at the last position, which is +1.405 on the clean prompt and −4.417 on the corrupted one (the model prefers neither capital as its top token, as lesson 09 showed, but it does prefer the right one of the two). Patched residual stream, blocks.L.hook_resid_post, every layer, every position. Reported as the fraction of the clean-minus-corrupted gap recovered.

Pre-registered: patching at the country token’s position recovers more than 50% of the gap at some layer; patching at position 0, the token “The”, which carries no information about which country, recovers less than 10% at every layer.

Read the two columns that move. At the country position (index 4, “ France”), patching any layer from 0 to 7 recovers essentially the whole gap, which says the country identity sits at that position in the residual stream through those layers. From layer 8 the recovery at position 4 falls and the recovery at the final position rises; by layer 11 patching the final position alone recovers everything. The information moves from the country token to the final token between layers 8 and 10, which is where attention must be carrying it. Lesson 09’s attribution put the largest attention contribution at block 9; patching makes that a causal statement rather than a projection.

The control column (position 0) is 0.00 at every layer, as pre-registered. Positions 1 to 3 (“The capital of”) are also zero, which was not pre-registered but is what the claim predicts.

What this does and does not establish

It establishes that the residual stream at the country position, up to layer 7, is sufficient to switch the model’s preference between Paris and Rome, and that by layer 11 the final position is sufficient. It does not establish which heads move the information (patch hook_z per head to find out), which MLPs convert country to capital (patch MLP outputs), or that the same structure holds for other country pairs (run them). It establishes nothing about why GPT-2 small’s top token is “ now”. A patching table is a map of where a difference travels, for one pair of prompts.

Choosing the pair

The clean/corrupted pair defines what “the information” means. France/Italy differ in the country and therefore the capital; they match in length, syntax, and every other token, so the only thing the patch can carry is the country. A pair that also differed in length, or in the determiner, would let the patch carry those too, and the recovery would be uninterpretable. The reference script asserts the two prompts tokenise to the same length, because a position-wise patch between different lengths is meaningless.

The lab

labs/patching.py asks for the model-free parts: recovered_fraction, a make_patch_hook that overwrites exactly one position in place and returns the tensor, and the verdict with claim and control positions. The tests check that the hook leaves every other position untouched and that the recovery metric is zero when the patch does nothing and one when it restores the clean metric.

In interpretability work

Interventional evidence is the standard for “uses.” Olsson et al. list direct ablation among their six arguments and are careful about its reach: “When we directly ‘knock out’ induction heads at test-time in small models, the amount of in-context learning greatly decreases” [Induction heads], with “in small models” doing real work in that sentence.

Pitfall

Patching with a corrupted input that is noise rather than a matched prompt. Replacing an activation with zeros or Gaussian noise takes the model off-distribution, and the recovery you measure is partly recovery from the damage. A matched corrupted prompt keeps every activation in distribution.

Check your understanding

Patching layer 3 at the country position recovers 99% of the gap. A colleague says “layer 3 computes the capital.” What does the table actually show about layer 3?

Reveal the reasoning

It shows that the residual stream at the country position after layer 3 contains whatever distinguishes France from Italy as far as the capital decision needs, which is also true after layer 0 (recovery 1.00). The country token’s embedding already carries that distinction; layers 0 to 7 preserve it at that position. The conversion to a capital happens somewhere the patch at position 4 stops working and the patch at position 5 starts, layers 8 to 10, and even that is a statement about where information moves, not what computes it.

Practice

Run the patching table, then implement labs/patching.py. Then change the pair to Spain/Portugal (Madrid/Lisbon), pre-register the same two predictions, and run.

python labs/reference/patch_run.py
python -m unittest discover -s labs -p 'test_patching.py' -v
What to look for

The pair must tokenise to the same length or the script will refuse. Before running, write what you expect the position-4 column to look like; if the structure differs from France/Italy, you have found that the mechanism is pair-specific, which is a result.
