Module 3 · The whole model

Generation and the KV cache

A language model becomes a text generator by sampling one token and appending it. The cache that makes this fast follows from the causal mask, and the output shows it is exact.

The loop

Generation is a loop around the forward pass: run the model on the prompt, take the logits at the last position, pick a token, append it, repeat. Greedy decoding picks the argmax every time; sampling draws from the softmax, optionally after dividing the logits by a temperature. Nothing in the model changes between steps; only the input grows.

python labs/anatomy.py generate
greedy:        'The cat sat on the mat, and the cat sat on'
T=1.0, seed 0: 'The cat sat on the mat along with seven other relatives and'
hand-rolled greedy with KV cache:    [11, 290, 262, 3797, 3332, 319]
hand-rolled greedy recomputing all:  [11, 290, 262, 3797, 3332, 319]
identical: True; cache holds 12 layers of (key, value), key shape (1, 12, 12, 64)

Greedy decoding from the running prompt produces ", and the cat sat on": a comma (the top token from the logit lens in lesson 09, probability 0.237), then a loop back into the prompt's own phrase. The sampled continuation with seed 0 at temperature 1.0 is different and is reproducible only with that seed. The six greedy token ids are printed twice, computed two ways, and they agree.

Temperature and the softmax

Dividing logits by a temperature T before the softmax changes the shape of the distribution without changing the ranking. Lesson 09's demo showed [2, 1, 0] becoming [0.665, 0.245, 0.090] at T = 1, flatter [0.507, 0.307, 0.186] at T = 2, and sharper [0.867, 0.117, 0.016] at T = 0.5. As T → 0 the softmax puts all its mass on the argmax, which is greedy decoding; T = 1 is the model's own distribution. Top-k and nucleus sampling truncate the distribution before drawing; the demo passes top_k=0 so that the only modification is the temperature.

Why caching works

Recomputing the whole sequence each step costs O(s²) attention work per step. The causal mask makes most of it redundant. Position j's key and value in every layer depend only on the residual stream at position j, which depends only on positions ≤ j. Appending a token at position s therefore does not change any key or value at positions 0 to s − 1, in any layer. So you can store them. At each new step you run only the new position through the model: compute its query, key, and value in each layer, attend from its query to the stored keys, append its own key and value to the store, and continue. The attention computation per step becomes O(s) and the rest of the block (layer norms, MLP, projections) runs on one position instead of s.

The demo does this by hand with use_cache=True and past_key_values, and in parallel recomputes the full sequence every step. The two lists of token ids are identical, which is the claim: caching changes the cost, not the result. The cache after six prompt tokens and six generated tokens holds, per layer, a key tensor of shape (1, 12, 12, 64): batch, heads, twelve positions, d_head. That is the (b, n_head, s, d_head) layout from lesson 11, grown by one position per step.

cache size = 2 × n_layer × b × n_head × s × d_head floats
= 2 × 12 × 1 × 12 × s × 64 = 18,432 × s floats for GPT-2 small

For a 1,024-token context that is about 19M floats, 75 MB in float32, per sequence: small for GPT-2, and the reason grouped-query attention (lesson 14) exists for large models, where the cache rather than the weights limits batch size.

What the cache is not

The cache stores keys and values, not the residual stream. If an interpretability experiment patches the residual stream at an earlier position during generation, the cached keys and values at that position are stale and the generation continues from the unpatched values. Libraries differ in whether they recompute; when in doubt, generate without a cache, or patch and re-run the prefix.

In interpretability work

Most interpretability experiments do not need generation at all: a single forward pass on a fixed prompt gives every activation and every logit. When a claim is about generated text ("the model continues with X"), it is really a claim about a sequence of forward passes, each conditioned on the previous choices, and the sampling policy (greedy, temperature, seed) is part of the experiment's specification. Record it.

Pitfall

Comparing a sampled continuation across runs or models without fixing the seed and the sampling parameters, then reading the difference as a difference in the models. The demo's sampled line changes with the seed; the greedy line does not. Use greedy decoding, or many samples with reported statistics, when the continuation is the measurement.

Check your understanding

During cached generation you zero out the layer-5 MLP output at position 2 of the prompt, then generate ten tokens. Which later computations see the change, and which do not?

Reveal the reasoning

If the prefix is run once with the intervention and its keys and values cached, every later position attends to the intervened keys and values at position 2 in layers 6 to 11 (and the layer-5 attention keys/values are unaffected because they were computed before the MLP). But the residual stream at position 2 is never recomputed, so nothing downstream of position 2 that depends on its post-intervention stream in layer 5 itself changes beyond what flows through keys and values. If instead the intervention is applied as a hook on every forward step but the cache was built without it, the cached keys and values at position 2 are stale and the generated tokens attend to the unintervened values. The experiment must say which.

Practice

Rerun with a lower temperature and another seed, then on a prompt with a strongly expected continuation. Watch whether the sampled line moves toward the greedy line.

python labs/anatomy.py generate --temperature 0.5 --seed 1
python labs/anatomy.py generate "The capital of France is" --tokens 4
What to look for

At temperature 0.5 the sampled continuation is more likely to coincide with the greedy one, since the distribution is sharper; with a different seed it may still differ. On the capital prompt the greedy continuation is whatever the model's argmax chain produces, printed exactly; the hand-rolled cached and uncached lists agree again, because the identity does not depend on the prompt.
