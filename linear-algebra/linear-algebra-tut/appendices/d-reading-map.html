The two papers cited

A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits is cited for three statements: the residual stream is a sum of layer outputs, it has no privileged basis, and the QK and OV products are low-rank. [Elhage et al. 2021] Toy Models of Superposition is cited for the definition of superposition and for the geometric fact about almost-orthogonal vectors. [Elhage et al. 2022] Read them after lesson 12; both assume exactly the material in this course and nothing more.
