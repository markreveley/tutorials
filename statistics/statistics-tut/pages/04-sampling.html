Module 1 · Understand uncertainty

Sampling: which world does the score describe?

A large sample only helps if its selection process connects it to the population you care about.

Population, frame, sample

The population is the world your claim addresses. The sampling frame is the accessible source you draw from. The sample is what you actually observe. For an assistant, the population might be next month’s user tasks, the frame last month’s eligible logged tasks, and the sample 500 randomly chosen records. Each transition introduces a question.

Are users who opted out absent from the frame? Did logging miss long sessions? Does next month introduce a new language? Statistical sampling uncertainty concerns variation inside a stated selection model. It does not by itself account for unrepresented users or future distribution shift.

Target: future eligible work ↓ approximation and time shift Frame: available, consented historical cases ↓ sampling policy Sample: selected cases ↓ exclusions and execution Analyzed data: recorded, scored outcomes

Random selection and deliberate coverage

Random sampling makes selection probabilities explicit. It does not guarantee that every small sample resembles the population. Stratified sampling divides the frame into categories and samples within each, which can ensure meaningful coverage of rare categories. If you oversample a category, you may need weights to estimate the original workload average.

A deliberately difficult challenge set serves a different purpose. It can reveal whether a failure is possible, exercise an important boundary, or track regressions. Its failure fraction is not automatically a prevalence estimate for production traffic. Preserve its value by naming its intended use.

For a small regression suite, a fixed-set report may be entirely appropriate: these exact cases passed or failed under these conditions. Avoid attaching a population interpretation that the selection process cannot support.

Why more data cannot remove every error

Suppose all your sampled tasks are English summaries, but your deployment also answers Spanish questions and edits code. A million summaries can estimate behavior on that summarization frame precisely. It cannot fill the missing task categories. More observations reduce random sampling variation under a model; they do not necessarily reduce selection bias.

Repeatedly taking fresh random samples from the same incomplete frame gives you repeated versions of the same omission. When a score matters, inspect both the uncertainty calculation and the frame’s coverage. These are separate pieces of evidence.

Separate development from confirmation

You may inspect development tasks, fix failure modes, and iterate. That is useful engineering. A held-out evaluation asks whether the changes transfer to cases that did not guide those choices. Split at the level that prevents leakage: repository, user, document, or template when its descendants are related. Randomly splitting individual paraphrases from the same source can leave near-duplicates on both sides.

Keep a manifest recording where tasks came from, selection rules, grouping, exclusions, and split assignment. If someone asks why a result should generalize, that manifest is often more useful than another decimal place.

In eval work

Build two explicitly named assets when needed: a representative sample for population estimates and a failure-focused suite for diagnostics. They can inform the same decision while answering different questions.

Pitfall

Calling a convenience sample “representative” because it contains many rows. Representativeness concerns how cases enter the dataset, not simply its size.

Check your understanding

You collect 1,000 examples by searching logs for “user complained.” What can the failure rate tell you?

Reveal the reasoning

It describes the selected complaint-linked cases under your labeling policy. Without a known selection mechanism relating those cases to all eligible interactions, it does not estimate the overall failure rate. It can still be excellent material for error analysis.

Practice

Design a sample for an agent serving routine and sensitive tasks. Specify a frame, a random selection rule, a grouping ID, and one likely coverage gap.

What to look for

A defensible answer names the accessible population and its omissions, preserves related-group IDs, and reserves independent groups for confirmation. If you sample equal numbers from unequal categories, state whether the final target is category-balanced or traffic-weighted.
