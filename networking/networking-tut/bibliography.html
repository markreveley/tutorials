How to read the evidence

Protocol facts are grounded in the textbook and specifications. Provider behavior is described with its provider name and official source. Eval architecture choices, retry budgets, incident scenarios, and the readiness rubric are reasoned teaching recommendations, not universal provider defaults or hiring criteria. The local lab verifies only the behavior of its own included server.
