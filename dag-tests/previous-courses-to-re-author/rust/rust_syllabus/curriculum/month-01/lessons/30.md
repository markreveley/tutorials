## Concept

**Checkpoint: evidence for system guarantees.** Defend the system's observed guarantees, demonstrate an unfamiliar failure, and prescribe the next foundation work from evidence.

## Today's path

The **guiding agent** is the agent helping you in this conversation; older records call this role the “supervisor.” You do not need another person or program.

Follow the sections in order. On return, resume the recorded stopping point without repeating finished predictions or reading. Complete this lesson's preflight at **Starting state**, then run exercise commands from `/home/ubuntu/syllabus` unless a step changes directories.

## Exercise

1. Walk through request → execution → outcome → evidence using your implementation and state who owns each important resource. Record the explanation in `labs/m1-30/month-review.md` before receiving corrections.

2. The guiding agent supplies two fresh bounded cases: one output/deadline/descendant case and one missing-evidence or namespace/resource-control case. Record predictions first, then use your existing reproduction commands. Supply actual outcomes and explain differences; no new subsystem is required. Any hints or fixes are recorded as assistance.

3. Revisit your M1-02 claims using the evidence index. For host trust, shared kernel, same-user file access, controller placement, and mutable logs, state what the demonstrations establish and what remains untested. Link evidence rather than copying previous notes. Update only product claims actually affected by the review.

4. In `labs/m1-30/next-boundary.md`, list unresolved foundations first. Then use the assigned Firecracker/jailer reading to propose the next learning boundary and its prerequisites. This future information is presented here because choosing the next stage is part of this checkpoint; it requires a proposal, not installing software, testing KVM, or renting hardware. The guiding agent maintains capability/status records based on the actual independent results.

Keep answers and results in the named checkpoint artifact. Under **Learner explanation**, briefly compare your result with your prediction and identify remaining uncertainty. Do not ask the agent to write the independent explanation.

## Independent understanding check

The walkthrough and two changed cases are the final independent check. Documentation lookup is allowed; agent-written explanations or fixes are recorded as assistance and require a fresh attempt.

The guiding agent records the case, your actual answer/change, observed result, any help, and its assessment under **Independent check**. A hint makes that attempt assisted; work through the gap, then try a different case. A successful command alone does not pass the check.

## Exit criteria

- The learner independently explains the implemented path and two unfamiliar cases.
- The final claims are linked to observed evidence and explicitly bounded.
- The next phase has concrete prerequisites rather than presumed completion of all foundations.

The guiding agent checks these against your files and answers, records supporting evidence under **Exit criteria**, and identifies anything missing. Completion also requires a passed independent check.
